Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is Adani Electricity's green tariff?

    Adani Electricity's green tariff is a specialized electricity plan that allows you to power your home or business with electricity generated from renewable sources. It's a sustainable and environmentally responsible way to meet your energy needs.

  • How does Adani Electricity's green tariff work?

    Our green tariff works by sourcing your electricity from renewable energy generators like solar or wind energy. When you opt for this tariff, you support the generation of clean energy, which is more eco-friendly.

  • What are the benefits of Adani Electricity's green tariff?

    Some of the benefits of green energy are-

    • It reduces your carbon footprint and supports clean energy.
    • It helps in the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions.
    • You can contribute to a more sustainable and eco-conscious future by switching to green energy.
  • How much can I save with Adani Electricity's green tariff?

    The savings can vary based on your consumption, but green tariffs can often be competitive with traditional tariffs in the long run. Plus, you get the added benefit of supporting clean energy and reducing environmental impact.

  • How do I know if Adani Electricity's green tariff is right for me?

    If you're environmentally conscious and want to make a positive impact on the planet, our green tariff is right for you. It's a great choice if you want to support renewable energy and reduce your carbon footprint.

  • How do I opt for Adani Electricity's green tariff?

    Switching to our green tariff is easy. You can simply click on the Apply Now tab on this page, fill in your details on the form, and we shall get in touch with you.

    By choosing our green tariff, you directly contribute to reducing the usage of harmful fossil fuels, leading to decreased greenhouse gas emissions and improved air quality. It's a meaningful step toward a cleaner and healthier environment.
