Change Name on Bill

With the advent of online services, applying for a change of name on your electricity bill is now a hassle-free process. To initiate the electricity bill name change, you can submit a name change application through the online platform. This streamlined process to change the name on your electricity bill allows you to avoid lengthy paperwork and in-person visits.

Ownership transfers made possible in a jiffy!

Instantly process your Name Change request by filling out the form.

Apply Now Check Status

Things to know before you apply

Clear Dues

Applicant need to clear all dues for existing CA Number before changing the name.

Upload Applicant Documents
  1. Self-attested photograph in JPEG format.
  2. Self-attested photo ID proof - any document (Pan card, Aadhar Card, Driving License, Election Card or passport as well as ownership document in (.jpg, .jpeg, .bmp, .gif, .tif format) - max size up to 5 MB)
  3. Applicant needs to upload self-attested scan copies of all documents.
Joint Ownership
  1. In the case of joint ownership, if the applicant requests to transfer to the sole name, they should submit consent from the co-owner with a self-attested Photo ID.
  2. You can also transfer the electricity bill to both names.
  3. The co-owner NOC can be downloaded from here. Click here
CA Number (Contract Account)

After the change of name, your old CA Number will be changed and the new CA Number will be allotted to the applicant.

E-Nach Service

The NACH facility will automatically be terminated for the old CA Number. The applicant has to apply online with New CA number. Click here. .

Demise Case

In case of the death of a registered consumer, the name change request can be processed by submitting documents. Click here for document requirements.

Applicant can opt for transfer of security deposit while change of name in blood relation through online portal.

Processing Fees

Processing Fee will be charged in the first bill from the date the change of name application has been processed.

Single phase - Rs.90

Three phase - Rs.295

Security Deposits

The consumer should maintain a security deposit after the change of name.

  1. As per MERC Regulations 2021 & clauses under 13.1 and 13.2 of the said Regulations all electricity consumers in the state are required to maintain a Security Deposit with their electricity distribution licensee, equivalent to twice the average of the last 12 months Bill amount (Recorded from April to March of immediately Precending Financial Year)
  2. For the transfer of the existing Security Deposit to the new account of the applicant, the applicant needs to submit a No Objection Letter from the present registered consumer along with a self-attested photocopy of his/her photo ID proof issued by the government authority.
  3. Format of transfer of Security Deposit. Download Now.

The applicant is requested to keep all the scanned copies of documents ready and follow the instruction to process the online change of name on website.

Premise Type

Users visiting the Adani electricity homepage can choose from two methods to access the online Electricity bill name change feature.

  • Click on “Services”, followed by the first dropdown option labelled “Change name on Bill,” OR Navigate to the quick links section on the same page, where you’ll locate the “Change name on Bill” tab.
  • Once you click on the link, you can view the application process and the specific document requirements for various types of premises.
  • Customers who have previously submitted their name change application can verify its status by clicking on the “Check Status” option.

Submit your application in four easy steps.

  1. Customers looking to initiate a new application can click on “Apply now” and subsequently provide their CA number and mobile number.  After verifying the OTP, the applicant can continue their application.
  2. Within the application, it is essential to complete all the necessary details corresponding to the chosen premise type.
  3. After completing the procedure, please accept the terms and conditions to continue and ensure that the uploaded document copies are self-attested.
  4. Then, click on “Submit Application”.
