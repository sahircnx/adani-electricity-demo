Smart Meter Evolution: From Analog to Digital Solutions

As the backbone of any power distribution network, electricity meters have undergone significant evolution over the decades. From the early days of mechanical meters to today’s smart meters, this evolution reflects the broader advancements in technology and the increasing demand for efficiency, accuracy, and data-driven insights.

We recognize the importance of these technological advancements in helping us serve our customers better. In this blog, we take a journey through the evolution of electricity meters and highlight how these changes have enhanced our ability to deliver reliable and efficient services.

Let’s take a look at the changes in meters over the years:


Year: 1954

Make: Ferranti

Feature: Electromechanical meter with dial-based counters

Year: 1955

Make: Ferranti

Feature: Electromechanical meter featuring two Discs and dial-based counter

Year: 1985

Make: Simco

Feature: Electromechanical meter featuring three Discs and rotor-based counter

Year: 1985

Make: Elymer

Feature: Electromechanical meter with rotor-based counters

Year: 1987

Make: Industrial Meters Pvt Ltd.

Feature: Electromechanical Trivector meter having three modes of measurement (KWH,KVARH & KVAH ) in a single meter.

Year: 1995

Make: Secure

Feature: Three-phase Static (ASIC-based) meter with an accuracy of class 1S with downloadable features of load survey, tamper indication, and history of consumption in downloaded data.

Year: 2001

Make: Landis & Gyr

Feature: An electromechanical meter with magnetic suspension, used to avoid frictional losses.

Year: 2002

Make: Actaris

Feature: Electromechanical meters with polycarbonate body instead of metallic body.

Year: 2003

Make: Landis and Gyr

Feature: Static Grid meter

Year: 2004

Make: Kaifa

Feature: Static (Controller + Energy Measuring chip) meter with an accuracy of class 1S.

Year: 2008

Make: Genus

Feature: Static meter with an accuracy of class 1 and with the feature of load survey and cover open tamper.

Year: 2009

Make: Landis & Gyr

Feature: Static meter with accuracy of class 1 and feature of battery backup under no voltage condition. (C- shaped current transducer)

The Era of Connectivity: Smart Meters

In recent years, the evolution of electricity meters has entered an exciting new phase with the introduction of smart meters. These devices represent the pinnacle of metering technology, combining advanced digital measurement capabilities.


Smart meters offer several groundbreaking features that are transforming the way electricity is managed and consumed. For instance, they enable real-time monitoring of electricity usage, allowing consumers to see exactly how much energy they are using at any given moment. This not only empowers consumers to make more informed decisions about their energy usage.

The evolution of meters is not just a story of technological progress; it’s a testament to our ongoing dedication to innovation, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
