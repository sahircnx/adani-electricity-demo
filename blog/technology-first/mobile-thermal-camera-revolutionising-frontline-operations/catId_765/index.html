Mobile Thermal Camera: Revolutionising Frontline Operations

Introduction

Adani Electricity has introduced a mobile thermal camera to empower frontline workers in identifying and addressing electrical issues proactively. This innovative technology promises to enhance operational efficiency, ensure customer satisfaction, and improve safety standards.

The Need for Advanced Technology

AEML manages an extensive asset base, including transformers, switchgears, capacitors, and LV panels. With the rise of multi-story high-rise projects, redevelopment activities, and shifting weather patterns, AEML's peak demand increases by approximately 150 MW annually. The peak demand is projected to reach 3263 MW, according to the 12th Electric Power Survey by the Central Electricity Authority (CEA). This surge in electricity demand subjects electrical assets to higher currents, leading to hot spots and, in some cases, fires.

To mitigate these risks, AEML previously deployed handheld IR thermal cameras from Fluke and FLIR during 2014-15. However, these cameras were costly, with high procurement and maintenance expenses (4-5 lakh INR). Consequently, their deployment was limited to a few executives, leaving field staff without the necessary tools to report and address hot spot abnormalities and developing faults.

Serving Customers Better

The Mobile IR Imager is a cost-effective IR imaging solution with adequate resolution (49k). These devices are 90% cheaper than conventional IR cameras and are compatible with both iOS and Android applications. Equipped with advanced features like Super IR, a digital camera (dual images IR+normal simultaneously), and auto-calibration, this device allows operators to capture, analyze, annotate, and forward IR and normal images on the fly. Early detection, reporting, and analysis of hot spots and equipment abnormalities ensure uninterrupted service for customers and reduce repair and maintenance costs, positively impacting tariffs.

Empowering Power Warriors

The Mobile IR Imager provides AEML's Power Warriors with direct avenues to inspect, analyze, and report abnormalities in a single visit, eliminating the need for multiple visits. This saves resources and additional man-hours required to address an abnormality. Empowered with the IR imager, Power Warriors can take immediate action to prevent failures and unplanned shutdowns. This sense of belonging and accomplishment enhances the company's brand image. Performance-linked incentives or rewards further motivate Power Warriors to contribute to this initiative.

Reducing Time and Improving Efficiency

Previously, inspection volumes were limited, with IR images downloaded at the end of the day and analyzed the next day. Abnormality planning took 2-3 days, considering the need for consumer consent for shutdowns. With the new technology, the team can identify problems in real-time, report, and address or arrange consumer shutdowns in a single visit. As inspection volumes increase, the number of abnormalities and potential failure cases will be proactively attended to.

Long-term Technological Development

In the long run, the solution will be integrated into the Mywork App workflow. This integration will enable teams to capture, annotate, and upload IR and normal images to the cloud. Using OCR and AI/ML, the images will be analyzed for potential anomalies, and separate workflows will be created in SAP or equivalent systems. This will aid in reporting, dashboarding, budget planning, and predictive analysis using trend analysis. Insights will be correlated with other applications like vigilance and meter reading to provide comprehensive business intelligence.

Deployment of Devices

A total of 200 IR imagers have been deployed, with 171 assigned to Fuseman, Operation Squad, and LT Teams.

Adani Electricity's mobile thermal camera is a game-changer for frontline operations, ensuring early detection and resolution of electrical issues. This technology not only enhances operational efficiency but also ensures customer satisfaction and safety, paving the way for a smarter and more reliable electrical infrastructure.
