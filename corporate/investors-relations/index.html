    Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited Brief Profile of Directors

    Name Brief Write up on the Director Photograph
    Mr. Anil Sardana Chairman Non-Executive Director

    Mr. Anil Sardana, aged 60, comes with over 3 decades of experience in the power and infrastructure sector. He started his career with NTPC and subsequently worked with BSES and Tata Group companies in the power and Infra sector, ranging from generation, power systems design, power distribution, Telecom and project management. Prior to joining the Adani Group, he was the MD & CEO of Tata Power Group based out of Mumbai.
    Mr. Anil Sardana does not hold any shares in Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited

    Mr. Kandarp Patel Managing Director and CEO

    Mr. Kandarp Patel, aged 47, holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering from Birla Viswakarma Mahavidhyalaya Engineering College and an MBA in Finance from G.H. Patel PG Institute of Business Management. Mr. Patel has a total of more than 17 years of experience in the areas of Power Trading, Fuel Management, Legal and Regulatory and Commercial aspects of the Power Business.
    Mr. Kandarp Patel does not hold any shares in Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited

    Mr. Sagar Adani Non-Executive Director

    Mr. Sagar Adani, aged 25, joined the Adani Group in 2015 after graduating in Economics from Brown University, USA. Having started off his career in Projects, he is credited with building the entire solar and wind portfolio of Adani Green Energy.
    Mr. Sagar Adani does not hold any shares in Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited

    Mr. Kalaikuruchi Jairaj Independent Director

    Mr. K. Jairaj was a member of the elite Indian Administrative Service (IAS), for over three decades until 2012. He had leadership assignments in public governance, energy, urban development, transport, finance and infrastructure areas, capping his illustrious career as Additional Chief Secretary, Government of Karnataka.
    He spearheaded the establishing of the Bangalore International Airport (BIAL), as Special Officer and Founder Managing Director. BIAL, had N R Narayanamurthy (Founder, Infosys Ltd) as Chair, and was India's first Greenfield Airport, under Private Public Partnership.
    He has worked in the World Bank, Washington D.C. as Senior Public Sector Management Specialist, Africa Region, focusing on Governance & budgeting in Nigeria, Ghana & Ethiopia.
    He is currently an Independent Director on the Boards of several publicly listed and Private Companies, in the Energy, Logistics, Infrastructure, Retail and Engineering Services, Packaged Food domains. He has served as Public Policy Advisor, E& Y; Manipal Group and mentors startups.
    Mr. K Jairaj does not hold any shares in Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited.

    Mrs. Chandra Iyengar Independent Director

    Mrs. Chandra Iyengar holds degree in MA from Miranda House, New Delhi and is an officer of the 1973 batch of the Indian Administrative Services (IAS). Over the course of her career, she has led several departments in the Government of Maharashtra and the Government of India, such as Women & Child Development, Higher & Technical Education, Rural Development, and Health. As the Secretary for Women & Child Development for the Government of Maharashtra, she was responsible for drafting and implementing the first-ever state policy for women’s empowerment in India.
    She has served as Additional Chief Secretary – Home Ministry for the Government of Maharashtra and as a Director on the Board of Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL) and also as a Chairperson for the Maharashtra Energy Regulatory Commission (MERC).
    Mrs. Chandra Iyengar does not hold any shares in Adani Electricity Mumbai Limited

    Mr. Quinton Choi Non Executive Director

    Mr. Quinton Choi is a Director at Qatar Investment Authority (‘QIA’). He has over 16 years of principal investing and investment banking experience in power, utilities and infrastructure. Prior to joining QIA in 2014, Quinton worked at Barclays and Dresdner Kleinwort. Quinton holds a Bachelor of Science Economics degree at the London School of Economics and Political Science.

    Mr. Kenneth McLaren Non Executive Director

    Mr. Ken McLaren is Chief, Investment Execution and a member of Executive Committee for QIA. Ken joined QIA in 2018 and has over 20 years of combined principal investing and investment banking experience. Prior to joining QIA, Ken was a Managing Director at Merrill Lynch in a variety of roles including Head of Infrastructure M&A in EMEA. Ken holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Hons.) from Queen’s University in Canada and is a Chartered Financial Analyst holder.
