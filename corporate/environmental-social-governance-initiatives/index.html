Our 3 fold approach towards ESG is as follows

Care for Planet Earth

Reducing carbon footprint. 30% procurement from renewable sources by FY 2023 and 60% procurement by FY 2027 Water and Waste management

Care for Society

Focus on Education Health, Sustainable Livelihood and Community Infrastructure. Providing high quality of reliable power supply

Care for Stakeholders

Major Milestones achieved till date

Always connected with customers & community for support:

Skill Development in local community

ADTPS has established tailoring training Centre at Dahanu. Till date 1,142 and in FY20 156 candidates trained and out of these 1142 and 1063 candidates respectively got employment

Mazi Sangini

Implemented Mazi Sangini in Mumbai to create a cadre of women entrepreneurs, who can become community leaders, and advisors to citizens on various topics like welfare, financial inclusion, women empowerment. The total applications made till now are 233 and the total benefits received are 123

Rural Infra & School development

Helped rural area & schools in developing infrastructure (such as girls toilets, water filters, medical checks by doctors, for improved health better quality of life

Electrification of Gorai village after 70 years

After acquisition, Adani commenced work on laying underground cables and using a substation to supply electricity in the remote area of Gorai to provide electricity to area without electricity for 70 years

Decarbonisation of India's Power Sector

Adani Electricity is deeply committed to the decarbonisation of power generation and to bringing green electricity to the residents of Mumbai.

Adani Electricity has invested in Green Distribution Infrastructure, including a capex program to ensure the higher reliability of power in Mumbai with a capital outlay of ~USD 3bn, funded through the company’s internal resources and a USD 2bn Global Medium Term Note program.

In total, this translates to a USD 3bn investment in green energy infrastructure for Mumbai.
