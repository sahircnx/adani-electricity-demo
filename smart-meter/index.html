Smart Meter

What is a Smart Meter

A smart meter is an electronic device that records your electricity consumption and communicates this information to both, you and the electricity provider, for monitoring and billing purposes. These meters have a SIM installed in it to communicate the energy consumption.

Benefits of Smart Meter

Manage your Consumption

Real-time monitoring helps you track and manage your energy consumption better.

Optimize your bill

Identify energy-saving opportunities with detailed usage data.

Relief from power thefts

Detects and deters electricity theft attempts.

Accurate billing

No more manual readings or estimated bills.

Receive timely alerts

Alerts for sudden usage spikes help you adjust your consumption pattern.

Quick issue resolution

Faster identification and resolution of issues like power outages.

Time of day (TOD) facility

Potential to reduce the overall bill by adjusting your appliance usage to off-peak hours when rates are lower.

Smart Meter Installation Process

Step1

Notification

Residents will be informed in advance about the installation schedule.

Step2

Scheduling

The electricity provider will coordinate with the housing society to install the meters within 60 days post the notification.

Step3

Installation

A professional installer will replace your meter with a smart meter, causing a brief 5-7 minute power interruption.

Step 4

Post Installation

The installer will provide a brief demonstration on how to read and monitor the smart meter.

Step 5

Support

Continuous support will be available through customer service for any questions or issues post-installation.

Myth

Smart Meters Increase Energy Bills

Fact

Smart meters provide accurate readings, eliminating estimation errors that can lead to overcharging.

Myth

Smart Meters Are Not Safe

Fact

Smart meters undergo rigorous testing and meet stringent safety standards set by regulatory bodies.

Myth

Smart Meters Are Not Safe

Fact

Smart meters only collect data on energy usage, not personal information or activities within the home.

Myth

Smart Meters Can Be Hacked Easily

Fact

Smart meters have advanced security measures to protect against hacking and unauthorized access.

Myth

Smart Meters Cause Health Issues Due to Radiation

Fact

Smart meters emit low levels of radiofrequency (RF) radiation, much lower than common devices like mobile phones and Wi-Fi routers, and are safe.

Evolution of Smart Meters

  • 2010
  • 1990
  • 1970
  • 1940

2010-PRESENT

Smart Meters
  • Introduction of SIM for communication
  • real-time data on electricity consumption, helping consumers to optimise energy use
  • Ensures accurate billing, facilitates quicker resolution of outages and accurate remote reading capability

1990-2000

Electronic Meters
  • Offering more precise measurements and improved billing accuracy
  • Were sensitive to electrical surges and spikes, which would damage the meter or affect its accuracy
  • Also vulnerable to hacking or tampering, if not properly protected

1970-1980

Advanced Electromechanical Meters
  • More accurate and reliable
  • Introduced to combat the issue of electricity theft which was rampant in India back then
  • They, too, were subject to wear and tear over time
  • Required timely maintenance, adding to the operational cost

1940-1960

Basic Electromechanical Meters
  • Simple devices with basic electricity measurement capability
  • Reading would be taken manually
  • Their mechanical components such as the spinning disk and gears would wear out over time and then affect accuracy
